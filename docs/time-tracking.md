# Time tracking

Open a project's **Time** tab.

## Log time

Press one of the buttons to add that time **right now**: **10 min, 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, 4 hours, 8 hours**. Each entry gets a timestamp.

Before you press, you can:

- write a **note** about what you did,
- pick a **to-do** to attribute it to (or leave it on *Whole project*),
- tick **Set date & time** to log work you did earlier.

Need something else? Enter hours and minutes and press **Add custom**.

You can also log time from anywhere with the [command palette](command-palette.md): type *log 30* and choose the entry.

## Review

- **Today**, **7 days** and **Total** at a glance, plus a 14-day chart.
- **History** groups entries by day. Hover an entry and use the bin to delete a mistake.

The dashboard card shows each project's total.
