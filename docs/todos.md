# To-dos and pages

Every project has its own to-dos. Open the **To-dos** tab.

## Pages

The left column lists where to-dos live:

- **All to-dos** shows everything.
- **General** holds to-dos that do not belong to a page.
- **Your pages**: create as many as you like with **New page** (for example *Roadmap*, *Bugs*, *Ideas*, *Exam prep*). Rename or delete a page from its header. Deleting a page moves its to-dos to General.

Each page has a **Markdown notes** area above its to-dos. It supports headings, lists, task lists, tables, links and code blocks. Choose **Edit**, **Split** or **Preview** at the top of the editor. Notes save automatically.

## To-dos

- Type in the box and press **Enter** to add one (to the selected page).
- Click the circle to mark it done, click the title or the arrow to open its details.
- Details: **status** (to do, in progress, done), **priority**, **due date**, and **Markdown notes**.
- Click the priority badge for a quick change.
- In **Manual** order, drag the handle on the left to reorder. **By priority** sorts for you.
- Completed to-dos are tucked under **Completed**.

## AI help

- **Prioritize with AI** orders the open to-dos in the current view and sets priorities. Hover a to-do afterwards to see the reason.
- **Generate** (in a to-do's details) writes a ready-to-paste prompt for an AI coding assistant, using the to-do and an overview of your repository. Copy it with one click.
